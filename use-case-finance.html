Deal material chaos

Pitch decks, CIMs, models, and board decks scattered across drives and email. Finding the right version means opening 10 folders and guessing which "final_v3" is actually final.

Searching for the right number

The revenue figure is in a model, the cap table is in a spreadsheet, and the board deck has a different number. You need the right one — fast — and you need to know where it came from.

Sending the wrong attachment

Attaching last quarter's deck instead of this quarter's costs credibility with investors and partners. The risk is invisible until it happens.

Ask Allybi "What was our Series B post-money valuation?" or "What is the fully diluted share count in the latest cap table?" and get the exact number with a citation to the source file, cell, or slide. No more opening five spreadsheets to cross-check.

Security posture for sensitive financial data.

Encryption by default

All data encrypted in transit and at rest. Financial models and cap tables are never exposed.

Grounded answers only

Numbers always cite the exact source file and cell. No fabricated figures.

No model training

Your financial data never trains AI models. This is contractually guaranteed in our terms.

Explicit send confirmation
