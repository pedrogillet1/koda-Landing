Work where your files and conversations already live.

Connect your email and messaging tools to search, draft, and send — with explicit confirmation before anything goes out.

Gmail

Search across your Gmail inbox and draft replies with the right file attached. Connect with OAuth, disconnect anytime.

Outlook

Every integration is designed with your privacy and control in mind.

Connect and disconnect anytime

One click to connect, one click to disconnect. No lock-in, no friction.

Permissioned access

OAuth scoped permissions mean Allybi only accesses what you authorize.

Explicit confirmation

Nothing is sent without your review. You see and approve every outgoing message.

No data retained after disconnect

Integration FAQ

Allybi currently supports Gmail, Outlook, and Slack integrations. You can also upload and edit .docx and .xlsx files directly in the workspace.

Go to your account settings and select the integration you want to connect. You'll be redirected to the service's OAuth page to authorize access with scoped permissions.

Allybi can draft emails, but nothing is ever sent without your explicit confirmation. You always see the full message, recipient, and attachments before approving.

When you disconnect an integration, Allybi immediately loses access to that service. No data from the disconnected service is retained in your workspace.
