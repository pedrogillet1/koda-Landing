About Allybi

We built Allybi for teams that cannot afford chaos or surveillance.

Sensitive work deserves tools that feel calm, clear, private, and trustworthy.

Why Allybi exists

Every day, professionals in legal, finance, and business handle hundreds of documents they did not create. Contracts arrive in different formats. Spreadsheets get duplicated. Slide decks proliferate across inboxes. Finding the right file at the right time becomes a tax on every decision you make.

Most AI tools promise to fix this, but they introduce a new problem: they require you to hand over your data with no transparency about how it is used, stored, or trained on. For teams working with sensitive information, that tradeoff is not acceptable.

Allybi was built to eliminate both problems. One workspace where you can search, compare versions, prepare responses, and send with source-cited answers and confirmation before anything goes out.

What we believe

Clarity over clutter

Every screen, every result, and every action should be immediately understandable. No feature bloat. No noise.

Control over black-box magic

You decide what gets shared, sent, or changed. AI assists; you confirm. No silent automation, no hidden side effects.

Evidence over vague output

Every AI answer cites its source. You can trace the reasoning, check the file, and verify before acting.

Calm over chaos

Good tools reduce anxiety, not add to it. Allybi is designed to feel quiet, organized, and predictable.

Speed without recklessness

Allybi helps you move faster -- but never at the cost of accuracy. Every answer is sourced, every action is verified, every send is confirmed.

Privacy by default

Your files are encrypted, isolated, and never used for model training. Privacy is not a feature toggle. It is the architecture.

What we optimize for

Speed to clarity. Answer with sources that cite sources. User control at every step. We measure success by how quickly you can move from question to confident action -- without sacrificing accuracy or privacy.

One private workspace where you can search across all your documents, compare versions, and take action -- all without switching between five different tools or worrying about where your data ends up.

What we will never do

Sell your data. Train models on your files. Hide where AI-generated answers come from. Send anything without your confirmation. Access connections you have not authorized.

These are not future commitments. They are current architecture decisions baked into how Allybi is built.

The kind of work Allybi is built for

Contract review and clause comparison. Regulatory filings where the wrong version can cost millions. Due diligence across hundreds of documents. Client communications that demand the right attachment, the right data, the right tone.

If your work involves sensitive files, high-stakes decisions, and time pressure, Allybi was built for you.
