About Allybi

We built Allybi for people who can't afford chaos or surveillance.

Sensitive work deserves tools that feel calm, clear, private, and trustworthy.

Why Allybi exists

Every day, professionals in legal, finance, and business handle hundreds of documents they did not create. Contracts arrive in different formats. Spreadsheets get duplicated. Slide decks proliferate across inboxes. Finding the right file at the right time becomes a tax on every decision you make.

Most AI tools promise to fix this, but they introduce a new problem: they require you to hand over your data with no transparency about how it is used, stored, or trained on. For teams working with sensitive information, that tradeoff is not acceptable.

Allybi was built to eliminate both problems. One workspace where you can search, organize, edit, and act on your documents, with AI that is grounded in your files and respects your privacy at every step.

The problem we refuse to accept

You should not have to trade privacy for speed.

Fast answers should not come at the cost of exposing your most sensitive files to opaque systems. Privacy and performance belong together.

You should not have to remember every filename to act quickly.

Searching by exact name is a relic. You should be able to ask for what you need in plain language and find it instantly.

AI should help without inventing certainty.

When AI answers your question, it should cite the source. When it does not know, it should say so. Grounded outputs are the only outputs worth trusting.

Product principles

Clarity over clutter

Every screen, every result, and every action should be immediately understandable. No feature bloat. No noise.

Control over black-box magic

You decide what gets shared, sent, or changed. AI assists; you confirm. No silent automation, no hidden side effects.

Evidence over vague output

Every AI answer cites its source. You can trace the reasoning, check the file, and verify before acting.

Calm over workflow chaos

Good tools reduce anxiety, not add to it. Allybi is designed to feel quiet, organized, and predictable.

Privacy by default

Your files are encrypted, isolated, and never used for model training. Privacy is not a feature toggle. It is the architecture.

Privacy and grounded outputs

Allybi grounds every AI response in the documents you have uploaded. This means answers come from your files, not from the open internet or from general model knowledge. When the AI references a clause, a number, or a date, you can trace it back to the exact source.

You control what files are included, what connections are active, and what actions the AI can take. Before anything is sent, shared, or changed, Allybi asks for your explicit confirmation. Nothing leaves your workspace without your approval.

This is not a privacy policy add-on. It is how the product is built.

What Allybi is building

One private workspace where you can search across all your documents, organize them by project or client, edit files directly, and take action, all without switching between five different tools.

Less time hunting for the right file. Fewer wrong-version mistakes. Faster, calmer work on the things that actually matter.

How we build

What we optimize for

Speed to clarity. Grounded answers that cite sources. User control at every step. We measure success by how quickly you can move from question to confident action.

What we will never do

Sell your data. Train models on your files without explicit consent. Hide where AI-generated answers come from. These are not future commitments. They are current architecture decisions.

How we make decisions

Privacy first. Evidence over opinion. Ship and iterate. Every feature ships with the question: does this make sensitive work calmer and more trustworthy?
