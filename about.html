From search to action.

Allybi doesn't just find your files — it helps you work with them. Two new capabilities, still private, still under your control.

New

AI Editing for Docs & Spreadsheets

Edit documents and spreadsheets directly through Allybi using natural language. No need to open another app.

  • Rewrite paragraphs and sections in .docx files
  • Update table data with simple instructions
  • Create spreadsheet formulas and calculations in .xlsx
  • Export changes back to your original file format
New

Outlook, Gmail & Slack Connections

Search across your messages and emails with natural language. Draft replies and attach the right document — without remembering the file name.

  • Search emails and messages using plain language
  • Draft and send with explicit confirmation before delivery
  • Attach files from Allybi (e.g. "attach the latest cap table")
  • Permissioned access — you choose what's connected
  • Disconnect anytime, no data retained

What we're building

Allybi exists to make working with documents feel calm again — no chaos, no surveillance, no complexity. Just clarity and control.

Mission

Give professionals, families, and teams a privacy-first AI that turns messy folders into instant, trustworthy answers — so they can spend their time thinking, not searching.

Vision

A world where everyone has calm, private control over their information: one place to ask anything they've worked on and get the right answer in seconds.

Values

Privacy first. Clarity over complexity. Respect your time. We build tools that are fast, honest, and never trade your data for convenience.
