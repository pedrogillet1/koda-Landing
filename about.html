What we're building

Koda exists to make working with documents feel calm again — no chaos, no surveillance, no complexity. Just clarity and control.

Mission

Give professionals, families, and teams a privacy-first AI that turns messy folders into instant, trustworthy answers — so they can spend their time thinking, not searching.

Vision

A world where everyone has calm, private control over their information: one place to ask anything they've worked on and get the right answer in seconds.

Values

Privacy first. Clarity over complexity. Respect your time. We build tools that are fast, honest, and never trade your data for convenience.

Why Koda?

Most tools force a tradeoff: convenience or privacy. Koda is built to give you both — without drowning you in complexity.
