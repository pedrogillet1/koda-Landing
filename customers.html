How professionals use Allybi in real pressure moments.

Legal Associate

Before partner review
Scenario

The partner asks what changed in the indemnity clause between the last two drafts. Twelve versions in the folder. The partner needs the answer before the call in ten minutes.

Pressure

20 minutes of folder diving, comparing documents side by side, version confusion. Risk of citing the wrong draft.

With Allybi

Asked "What changed in the indemnity clause between v2 and v3?" Found the change in 15 seconds, cited to Section 8.3(b), page 14. Sent the correct version to the partner with confirmation before send.

Found the exact clause change in 15 seconds. Sent the right version with confirmation.

Finance Analyst

Before board meeting
Scenario

The CFO needs Q4 ARR from the latest board deck. Three versions exist across OneDrive and email. The board meeting starts in 30 minutes.

Pressure

Searching across slide decks, risk of pulling a number from the wrong version. The wrong figure in front of the board costs credibility.

With Allybi

Asked "What was Q4 ARR in the latest board deck?" Found the exact figure — $4.2M — cited to slide 7 of Board_Deck_Q4.pdf. Prepared the email to the CFO with the deck attached and confirmed before send.

Found the exact figure with slide reference. Prepared email. Confirmed before send.

Cross-source search found the right file across email and folders. Identified the latest version (Acme_SOW_v2.docx). Prepared the WhatsApp handoff with the right file — reviewed content and recipient before opening WhatsApp.
