Security

Security and privacy, made visible.

Allybi is built for sensitive document workflows: grounded answers, permissioned connections, and confirmation before anything goes out.

Built on six trust pillars

Encryption at rest and in transit

All data is encrypted with TLS in transit and AES-256-GCM at rest. Your documents are protected at every stage of their lifecycle.

No training on user documents

Your documents are never used to train AI models. This is contractually guaranteed in our terms of service -- your data stays yours.

Permissioned integrations

Every connection requires explicit OAuth authorization. You choose exactly which services Allybi can access, and you revoke access anytime.

Source-cited answers

Every AI answer references the exact document, page, and section. If the answer is not in your documents, Allybi tells you instead of guessing.

Explicit confirmation before every send

Nothing leaves Allybi without your review. Every email, message, and file share requires explicit confirmation before it is sent.

Isolated workspaces

Projects are isolated. No data leakage between workspaces. Each workspace is encrypted and access-controlled independently.

What Allybi will and will not do

What Allybi will do

  • Cite sources for every answer -- file, page, and section
  • Require your explicit confirmation before anything is sent
  • Keep your data private, encrypted, and isolated

What Allybi will not do

  • Train models on your files
  • Send anything without your review and approval
  • Access connections you have not authorized
