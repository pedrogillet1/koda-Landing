Security

Security and privacy, made visible.

Allybi is built for sensitive workflows, grounded outputs, and user control. Here's how we protect your work.

Built on six trust pillars

Encryption at rest and in transit

All data is encrypted with TLS in transit and AES-256-GCM at rest. Your documents are protected at every stage of their lifecycle.

Grounded outputs from your files

Every AI answer cites the specific file and location. Allybi never fabricates information — if the answer isn't in your documents, it tells you.

Permissioned integrations

Every connection requires explicit OAuth authorization. You choose exactly which services Allybi can access, and you revoke access anytime.

Explicit confirmation before send

Nothing leaves Allybi without your review. Every email, message, and file share requires explicit confirmation before it is sent.

User control — disconnect anytime

You stay in control of every connection. Disconnect any integration instantly and your linked data is no longer accessible to Allybi.

No training on user documents

Your documents are never used to train AI models. This is contractually guaranteed in our terms of service — your data stays yours.

Data handling principles

Encryption in transit

All data uses TLS encryption between your device and Allybi servers. No unencrypted connections are ever permitted.

Encryption at rest

Documents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM at rest. Encryption keys are managed separately from data storage.

Grounded responses

AI answers cite specific files and locations. If the answer isn't in your documents, Allybi tells you instead of guessing.

No model training

User documents are not used to train AI models. This commitment is contractually guaranteed in our terms of service.

Integration security

Every connected service is permissioned, controllable, and transparent.

Permissioned connections

Each integration uses OAuth with scoped permissions. Allybi only accesses what you explicitly authorize — nothing more.

Disconnect controls

Revoke any integration instantly from your settings. Once disconnected, Allybi can no longer access that service's data.

Explicit send confirmation

Before any email or message is sent through a connected service, you see exactly what will be sent and confirm it yourself.
