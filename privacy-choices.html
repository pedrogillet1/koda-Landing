Privacy Choices

Effective Date: April 30, 2026

Your privacy rights and how to exercise them.

1. Cookie Preferences

Manage your cookie preferences through our cookie preference center. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies at any time.

View Cookie Policy

2. Analytics Opt-Out

You can disable analytics cookies through our cookie preference center. When disabled, Allybi will not collect analytics data about your usage of the Service.

3. Marketing Unsubscribe

Use the unsubscribe link included at the bottom of any marketing email to opt out of marketing communications. You will continue to receive transactional and account-related emails.

4. Data Access Request

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. To submit a data access request, email info@allybi.co.

5. Correction Request

If your personal data is inaccurate or incomplete, you can update it in your account settings or email info@allybi.co to request a correction.

6. Deletion Request

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. Email info@allybi.co or visit our Data Deletion page for detailed instructions.

7. Objection / Restriction

You may object to or request restriction of certain processing of your personal data. To exercise this right, email info@allybi.co with details about which processing you wish to object to or restrict.

8. Integration Disconnect

You can disconnect any third-party integration at any time. Go to Settings > Integrations and click Disconnect next to the service you wish to remove. See our Integrations and Data Use page for more details.

9. Account Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your Allybi account and all associated data. Email info@allybi.co with the subject “Data deletion request.”

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at info@allybi.co.
