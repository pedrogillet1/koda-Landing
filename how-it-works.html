How Allybi works

From upload to action -- a calmer, faster way to work with documents.

Organize

Edit

Step 1

Bring in the files that matter

Upload documents, decks, spreadsheets, and contracts. Allybi supports .docx, .xlsx, .pdf, .pptx, and more.

Group files by project, client, case, or deal. Your workspace structure mirrors how you actually work, not how a filing system thinks you should.

Acme Corp / Q4 Deal
NDA_v3_final.docx
Pricing_Model.xlsx
Board_Deck_Oct.pptx
Compliance / Annual Review
Audit_Report_2024.pdf
Step 2

Ask in plain language. Get grounded answers.

Type a question the way you would ask a colleague. Allybi searches across all your uploaded files and returns answers grounded in your documents, not general knowledge.

Every answer cites its source. Click to see the exact paragraph, cell, or slide the information came from. No hallucinations. No invented certainty.

"What is the non-compete clause in the Acme NDA?"
Grounded answer

Section 4.2 of NDA_v3_final.docx states: "Employee agrees not to engage in competing business activities for a period of 12 months following termination..."

NDA_v3_final.docx, page 3, section 4.2
Step 3

Always know which version you're working with.

When multiple versions of a file exist, Allybi tracks them automatically. You can compare changes between versions, see what was added or removed, and always know you are working from the latest draft.

No more opening three files to figure out which is the "real" one. Version awareness is built into search, editing, and every action you take.

v1 Contract_draft.docx Oct 3
v2 Contract_draft_v2.docx Oct 8
v3 Contract_final.docx Oct 12 Latest
Step 4

Edit documents without leaving Allybi.

Rewrite paragraphs in .docx files. Create formulas and update tables in .xlsx spreadsheets. Make changes directly inside Allybi without downloading, editing externally, and re-uploading.

AI-assisted editing suggests improvements, but you always review and approve before changes are saved. Every edit is traceable.

Proposal_Q4.docx Editing

"The proposed timeline is approximately 6-8 weeks depending on various factors."

AI suggestion

"The proposed timeline is 6 weeks, with a 2-week buffer for regulatory review. Milestones are outlined in Appendix B."

Step 5

Search across your messages.

Connect Gmail, Outlook, or Slack to bring your messages into the same workspace as your files. Search across emails and channels alongside your documents.

Access is permissioned. You control exactly which accounts and channels are connected. Disconnect anytime, and your data is removed from the workspace.

Step 6

Nothing leaves without your approval.

Before any email is sent, any file is shared, or any action is taken outside your workspace, Allybi shows you exactly what will happen and asks for your explicit confirmation.

This is not optional. It is the core design principle. You are always in control of what leaves your workspace.

Confirm before sending
To: sarah@acmecorp.com
Subject: Q4 Proposal - Final Version
Attachment: Proposal_Q4_final.docx

Security at every step.

Your documents are protected from the moment they enter Allybi to the moment you choose to act on them.

Encryption

Files encrypted at rest and in transit. Your data is isolated and inaccessible to other users or systems.

Grounded outputs

AI answers are sourced from your files. Every response includes citations so you can verify before you act.

Permissioned connections

You control which accounts are connected. Revoke access anytime. Data is removed when you disconnect.

Explicit confirmation

Nothing is sent, shared, or changed without your review and approval. Every outbound action requires confirmation.
