See what changed between drafts — instantly.

Upload two versions of any contract and Allybi highlights exactly what changed — clause by clause. Compare indemnification language, liability caps, or termination provisions without manual redlining. Every difference is surfaced with full context so you can brief your team in seconds, not hours.

Search clauses across every document in your matter.

Ask Allybi a natural-language question — "What are the termination provisions across all vendor contracts?" — and get grounded answers with exact citations. Every result links back to the source document, page, and passage. No fabricated answers. No missing context.

"What are the non-compete terms in the Anderson agreement?"
Anderson_MSA_v4.pdf — Page 12, Section 9.3
The Contractor agrees not to engage in any competing business activities within a 50-mile radius for a period of 24 months following termination of this Agreement.
Anderson_Amendment_2.pdf — Page 3, Section 2.1
The non-compete period described in Section 9.3 is hereby amended to 18 months with geographic scope reduced to 25 miles.
