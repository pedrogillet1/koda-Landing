Everything you need to know about Allybi, organized by topic.

Allybi is a private AI workspace for sensitive document work. It brings files, cloud folders, email context, and message handoff into one place -- so you can search, compare versions, prepare responses, and send with source-cited answers and confirmation before anything goes out.

Upload two or more versions of a document and ask Allybi what changed. It identifies specific differences -- clauses, numbers, dates, language -- and cites the exact page and section in each version.

Allybi supports PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, and common text formats. You can upload files directly or import them from connected integrations like OneDrive or SharePoint.

Outlook, OneDrive, and SharePoint are live now. WhatsApp is ready for message handoff. All connections use OAuth with scoped permissions.

Gmail and Google Drive are coming soon. We prioritize integrations based on customer needs during guided onboarding.

Allybi helps you prepare a message for the right contact -- you review the content and recipient, then Allybi opens WhatsApp with everything ready. It is a review-before-send handoff, not a synced inbox connection.
