# Marketing Audit — Allybi Landing Site

**Date:** 2026-03-31
**Scope:** 18 pages, 3-language i18n (EN, PT-BR, ES), component-based design system

---

## Current State

The site is well-built technically — clean components, complete i18n infrastructure, strong trust messaging, and specific use-case scenarios. The design system is consistent and professional.

## Key Issues

| Area | Problem |
|------|---------|
| **Positioning** | Equal weight given to legal, finance, and business. No clear primary audience. |
| **Visuals** | Zero humans in any imagery. Feels abstract and impersonal for a trust-dependent product. |
| **CTA confusion** | Mixed calls-to-action: "Request demo", "Book demo", "Start free" appear interchangeably. Undermines conversion clarity. |
| **Testimonials** | Labeled "Representative workflow" — signals fabricated social proof. Damages trust. |
| **Language default** | EN is default despite Brazilian lawyers being the primary market. PT-BR feels secondary. |
| **Pricing vs. launch model** | Pricing page shows self-serve tiers, but actual go-to-market is demo-led. Creates expectation mismatch. |

## Strengths

- Strong trust messaging throughout (encryption, human control, grounded AI)
- Specific scenario descriptions that resonate with real workflows
- Clean, reusable component library
- Complete i18n with translations in place

## Recommendations

1. **Lawyer-first positioning** — Lead with legal use cases. Demote finance/business from primary navigation to secondary or future sections.
2. **Simplified navigation** — Reduce top-level nav items. Legal should be the hero vertical.
3. **Unified CTAs** — One primary CTA everywhere: "Agendar demonstracao" (PT-BR) / "Book demo" (EN). Remove "Start free" and "Join waitlist".
4. **Default to PT-BR** — Geo-detect or default to Portuguese for Brazilian visitors.
5. **Honest workflow labels** — Replace "Representative workflow" with real case studies or remove testimonials until genuine ones exist.
6. **Demote finance/business** — Move to a "Coming soon" or secondary page rather than equal nav presence.
