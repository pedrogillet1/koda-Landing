# CTA Consolidation Map

---

## Active CTAs

### Primary — Book Demo
| Language | Label | Destination |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| EN | **Book demo** | `request-demo.html` |
| PT-BR | **Agendar demonstracao** | `request-demo.html` |

Usage: One per section. Always the most prominent button. Solid fill, brand color.

### Secondary — See How It Works
| Language | Label | Destination |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| EN | **See how it works** | `#how-it-works` (scroll anchor) |
| PT-BR | **Ver como funciona** | `#how-it-works` (scroll anchor) |

Usage: Paired with primary CTA in hero sections only. Outlined/ghost button style.

### Utility — Sign In
| Language | Label | Destination |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| EN | **Sign in** | `app.allybi.co/login` |
| PT-BR | **Entrar** | `app.allybi.co/login` |

Usage: Top-right nav only. Text link or minimal button. Never competes with primary CTA.

## Removed CTAs

| Former label | Reason for removal |
|--------------|--------------------|
| "Start free" | Conflicts with demo-led launch model. Creates expectation of self-serve access that doesn't exist yet. |
| "See customer stories" | No real customer stories exist. Linking to fabricated testimonials damages trust. |
| "Join waitlist" | Mixed signal alongside "Book demo". One conversion path is better than two. |
| "Request demo" | Consolidated into "Book demo" for consistency. Same destination, clearer language. |

## Rules

1. **One primary CTA per section.** Never stack two equally prominent buttons.
2. **Consistent labels across all pages.** "Book demo" everywhere, not "Request demo" on one page and "Get started" on another.
3. **Primary CTA in nav is always visible.** Sticky header keeps it accessible on scroll.
4. **Final section of every page** ends with the primary CTA and a single supporting line of text.
5. **No CTA in the footer.** Footer is for navigation and legal links only.
